# Projects

## Getting more customers

We tend to do well with small companies. They are often startups / scale-ups
that have a hard time recruiting the people they need; they might have tried
doing it themselves before, but it takes too much time out of their already
busy schedule. This creates a downward spiral where they have too much work to
do, so they have to hire someone, but hiring creates even more work.

Since I have a technical background, we also do well when people are trying
to recruit for niche technical roles, e.g. functional programming.

See <./prospects.md> for a list of the prospects I'm responsible for.
