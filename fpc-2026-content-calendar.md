### 2026-08-04 Speaker announcement

What if your compiler could help your AI agent produce better code?

We’re excited to welcome Adam Warski to Func Prog Conf 2026!

Adam is the co-founder of SoftwareMill, now part of VirtusLab, and an active open-source contributor to projects including Ox, Jox, Tapir and sttp.

In his talk, “The Scala 3 Features for Agentic Development, Today and Tomorrow,” Adam will explore how Scala 3’s type system can give coding agents faster feedback and make invalid programs impossible to generate.

He’ll cover practical features you can use today, such as opaque types and enums, as well as newer work including capture checking and Safe Scala.

Join us in Stockholm on October 14 for a practical look at what strongly typed functional programming can bring to agentic software development!

### 2026-08-12 Speaker announcement: Adam Tornhill

What can ten years of building a product in Clojure teach us about software evolution?

We’re excited to welcome Adam Tornhill to Func Prog Conf 2026!

Adam is a programmer, author, and the founder and CTO of CodeScene. Combining backgrounds in engineering and psychology, he develops new ways to understand codebases, technical debt, and the human side of software development.

In his talk, “Transforming Software Evolution: Lessons from 10 Years of Clojure in Production,” Adam will share how Clojure’s metaprogramming, embedded domain-specific languages, parallelism, and multi-methods helped a small Swedish company compete with teams 20 times its size.

He’ll also explore how language choices can shape productivity, innovation, and engineering culture—and how agentic coding fits into the picture.

Join us in Stockholm on October 14th to hear what a decade of Clojure in production can teach us about building and evolving software!

🎟️ Get your ticket: https://funcprogconf.com/

### 2026-08-14 2025 talk: Elisabeth Stenholm

How do you write concurrent code that is safe and composable—without managing locks?

In her talk from last year’s Func Prog Conf, Elisabeth Stenholm introduces Software Transactional Memory and shows how it can be used to write lockless concurrent code with Haskell’s STM monad.

Elisabeth explains how STM works through practical code examples, discusses its strengths and weaknesses, and compares it with traditional lock-based concurrency.

### 2026-08-31 Speaker announcement: Christoph Neumann

What happens when we view software development as an infinite game?

We’re excited to welcome Christoph Neumann to Func Prog Conf 2026!

Christoph is a programming fanatic, entrepreneur, and developer advocate for the Clojure team. His work has taken him into high-stakes industries including manufacturing and live sports production, powering projects for Hewlett Packard, the NFL, Wimbledon, Call of Duty, and The Overwatch League.

In his talk, “Infinite Coding,” Christoph will explore how treating software development as an infinite game changes the way we think about success, cost, and our ability to keep evolving software over time.

He’ll also introduce foundational principles that shaped Clojure—and show how they can help create successful, long-lived codebases while making development more sustainable for the people maintaining them.

Join us in Stockholm on October 14th to learn how functional programming and immutability can help us build software for the long run!

### 2026-09-04 Speaker announcement: Giuseppe Castagna

What does it take to bring a type system to a dynamically typed language like Elixir?

We’re excited to welcome Giuseppe Castagna to Func Prog Conf 2026!

Giuseppe is a Senior Research Scientist at CNRS and Université Paris Cité, and the director of IRIF. His research spans programming languages and type theory, with a particular focus on set-theoretic types.

He led the research behind Elixir’s new gradual set-theoretic type system, working with Guillaume Duboc and Elixir creator José Valim to bring types to the language without losing the flexibility that makes Elixir Elixir.

We’ll share more about Giuseppe’s talk soon. In the meantime, you can watch “Bringing Types to Elixir,” his ElixirConf EU 2023 presentation with Guillaume Duboc:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gJJH7a2J9O8

Join us in Stockholm on October 14th to hear from one of the researchers shaping the future of Elixir!
